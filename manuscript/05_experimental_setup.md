# Experimental Setup

## Dataset

All inputs are canonical, deterministic San Monique datasets embedded in the
pure domain modules:

- **Supply graph** (`supply_graph.py`): {{LALD_SUPPLY_NODES}} nodes and
  {{LALD_SUPPLY_EDGES}} directed legs with integer capacities; production crop
  ceilings and market demand ceilings bound the flow problem.
- **Communiqué** (`coded_comms.py`): a keyword cipher over the 26-letter
  alphabet with the fixed keyword {{LALD_KEYWORD}} and the canonical plaintext
  per the film's voodoo motif.
- **Terrain** (`island_ops.py`): a fixed 9x9 terrain grid of beaches, open
  ground, mangrove, swamp, and a crocodile channel walled by impassable cliff.

## Determinism

No random draws and no wall-clock dependence enter any analytic result. The
mission records a fixed provenance seed (8), a canonical input hash
(`{{LALD_INPUT_HASH}}`), and a zero wall-clock figure. Route tie-breaking,
Dijkstra ordering, and flow augmenting-path selection are all order-stable.

## Figures

Figures are regenerated by `scripts/generate_figures.py` under
`output/figures/` from the same pure-domain data the manuscript numbers are
computed from — no figure metric is hand-authored.
